## Possible document pairs (translations)

We do not expect to have duplicate documents in the corpus: the document
should be in the corpus in it's original language, and English-language
translations of the document should NOT also be in the corpus. 

However, we clearly have a few such cases right now: esp `zh` documents with
`en` twins.
